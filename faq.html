Answers to common questions about our services and policies.

General Inquiries

What areas do you serve? +

Monarch Property Care proudly serves the entire Greater Toronto Area (GTA), including Toronto, North York, Etobicoke, Scarborough, Mississauga, Oakville, Richmond Hill, Markham, and Vaughan.

Are you insured and WSIB compliant? +

Yes, absolutely. We carry $2M in comprehensive liability insurance and all our staff are fully covered under WSIB. We prioritize safety and professionalism in every job we undertake.

How do I get a quote? +

Getting a quote is simple! You can fill out the form on our Contact page, call us directly, or send an email. For standard services like window cleaning or gutter cleaning, we can often provide an estimate over the phone or via email using satellite imagery. For larger projects like deck restoration, we may schedule a quick, free site visit.

Do I need to be home during the service? +

For exterior services (window cleaning, pressure washing, eavestrough cleaning, snow removal), you do not need to be home as long as we have access to the water supply and the work areas (e.g., unlocked gates). For interior window cleaning, we require someone to be present to grant access.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Interac e-Transfer, and cash. Payment is typically due upon completion of the service to ensure your complete satisfaction.

What is your satisfaction guarantee? +

We stand behind our work 100%. If you are not completely satisfied with any aspect of our service, please let us know within 24 hours, and we will return to make it right at no extra cost.

Window Cleaning

Does your window cleaning service include screens and tracks? +

Yes! Our premium window cleaning service includes wiping down the frames and sills. Screen cleaning and deep track detailing can be added to your package for a complete restoration.

Can you remove hard water stains from my windows? +

In most cases, yes. Hard water stains caused by sprinklers or mineral deposits can often be removed with specialized solutions. Severe etching may require restoration services, which we can assess during a free consultation.

How often should I have my windows professionally cleaned? +

For most homes, we recommend professional window cleaning 2-4 times per year, depending on your surroundings (trees, construction, traffic). Homes near major roads or with lots of trees may benefit from more frequent cleaning.

Eavestrough & Gutter Cleaning

How often should I clean my eavestroughs? +

We recommend cleaning eavestroughs at least twice a year: once in late spring (after tree seeds and blossoms fall) and once in late autumn (after leaves have dropped). Homes surrounded by many trees may require more frequent cleaning.

What happens if my eavestroughs are clogged? +

Clogged gutters can cause water to overflow and damage your roof, fascia, siding, and foundation. In winter, ice dams can form, leading to leaks and costly repairs. Regular cleaning prevents these issues.

Pressure Washing

Is pressure washing safe for all surfaces? +

Not all surfaces are suitable for high-pressure washing. We assess each job individually and adjust our equipment and techniques accordingly. For delicate surfaces like painted wood or older brick, we use softwash methods with lower pressure and specialized cleaning solutions.

Will pressure washing damage my plants or lawn? +

We take great care to protect your landscaping. We pre-wet plants and rinse them after using any cleaning solutions. Our eco-friendly products are biodegradable and plant-safe.

Deck Cleaning & Staining

How long does deck staining take to dry? +

Most stains are dry to the touch within 24-48 hours, depending on weather conditions. We recommend waiting at least 48-72 hours before placing furniture back on the deck and avoiding heavy foot traffic during this time.

How often should I restain my deck? +

This depends on the type of stain, sun exposure, and foot traffic. Generally, transparent stains last 1-2 years, semi-transparent stains 2-3 years, and solid stains 3-5 years. We can assess your deck and recommend the best maintenance schedule.

Snow Removal

What time do you start clearing snow? +

Our crews begin work early in the morning (typically starting at 5 AM) to ensure your property is cleared before you need to leave for work. For heavy snowfalls, we may make multiple passes throughout the day.

Do you offer seasonal contracts? +

Yes! We offer both per-visit and seasonal contract options. Seasonal contracts provide peace of mind with a fixed price for the entire winter season, regardless of how many snowfalls we receive. Contact us for details.
