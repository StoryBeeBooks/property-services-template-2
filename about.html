Who We Are

Our Story

Founded with a vision to bring a higher standard of professionalism to the property maintenance industry, Monarch Property Care has grown from a small local operation to a trusted partner for homeowners and businesses across the Greater Toronto Area.

We understand that your property is one of your most significant investments. That's why we don't just clean; we care. Our team is trained to identify potential issues before they become costly repairs, ensuring your home or business looks its best and functions perfectly year-round.

At Monarch, we believe in transparency, reliability, and results that speak for themselves. Every job we take on is an opportunity to exceed expectations.

Safety & Certification
